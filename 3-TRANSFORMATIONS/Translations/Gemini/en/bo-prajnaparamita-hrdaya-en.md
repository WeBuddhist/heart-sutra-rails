## Main Body ^2-0

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-1]]

Then, by the power of the Buddha, the venerable Śāriputra said this to the bodhisattva mahāsattva noble Avalokiteśvara: ^2-1

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-2]]

"How should any son of noble family train who wishes to practice the profound practice of the perfection of wisdom?" When he had said this, the bodhisattva mahāsattva noble Avalokiteśvara said this to the venerable Śāradvatīputra: "Śāriputra, any son of noble family or daughter of noble family who wishes to practice the profound practice of the perfection of wisdom should observe in this way: they should correctly and repeatedly observe those five aggregates also as empty of inherent nature. ^2-2

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-3]]

Form is empty; emptiness is form. ^2-3

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-4]]

Emptiness is not other than form; form also is not other than emptiness. ^2-4

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-5]]

In the same way, feeling, discrimination, compositional factors, and consciousness are empty. ^2-5

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-6]]

Śāriputra, therefore all phenomena are emptiness; they are without characteristics, unborn, unceasing, stainless, not free from stain, undiminishing, and unfilled. ^2-6

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-7]]

Śāriputra, therefore, in emptiness there is no form, no feeling, no discrimination, no compositional factors, no consciousness; no eye, no ear, no nose, no tongue, no body, no mind; no form, no sound, no smell, no taste, no object of touch, and no phenomena. ^2-7

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-8]]

There is no eye element, up to no mind element, and up to no mind consciousness element. ^2-8

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-9]]

There is no ignorance, no exhaustion of ignorance, up to no aging and death, and up to no exhaustion of aging and death. There is no suffering, origin, cessation, and path; no exalted wisdom, no attainment, and also no non-attainment. ^2-9

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-10]]

Śāriputra, therefore, because bodhisattvas have no attainment, they rely on and abide in the perfection of wisdom. ^2-10

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-11]]

Since their minds are unobscured, they have no fear. Passing completely beyond error, they reach the ultimate end of nirvāṇa. ^2-11

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-12]]

All the buddhas who abide in the three times also fully awaken to unsurpassed, completely perfect awakening by relying on the perfection of wisdom. ^2-12

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-13]]

Therefore, the mantra of the perfection of wisdom, the mantra of great knowledge, the unsurpassed mantra, the mantra equal to the unequaled, the mantra that thoroughly pacifies all suffering, should be known as truth since it is not false. The mantra of the perfection of wisdom is proclaimed: ^2-13

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-14]]

tadyathā oṃ gate gate pāragate pārasaṃgate bodhi svāhā ^2-14

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-15]]

Śāriputra, a bodhisattva great being should train in the profound perfection of wisdom in that way. ^2-15

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-16]]

Then the Blessed One arose from that concentration and commended the bodhisattva great being, the noble Avalokiteśvara, saying: 'Well done, well done! Son of noble family, it is exactly so.' ^2-16

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-17]]

Son of noble family, it is exactly so. One should practice the profound perfection of wisdom just as you have taught, ^2-17

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-18]]

and the tathāgatas will rejoice. ^2-18

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-19]]

When the Blessed One had said this, ^2-19

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-20]]

venerable Śāriputra, the bodhisattva great being Avalokiteśvara, that entire retinue, and the world with its gods, humans, asuras, and gandharvas rejoiced and highly praised what the Blessed One had spoken. ^2-20

![[bo-prajnaparamita-hrdaya#^2-21]]

The Noble Heart of the Perfection of Wisdom is complete. ^2-21
