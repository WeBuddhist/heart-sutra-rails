# Heart Sūtra — outline from the Wikisource Index (Derge)

The table of contents of the Derge Kangyur Heart Sūtra on Wikisource (Index page, revision 1228972). Its proofread pages carry each heading inline, immediately before the text it opens; that text was found, letter for letter, at the start of a row of the display Tibetan. Row 1 — the document's own title line — precedes the first heading and stays `^0-1`.

Section 3, མཇུག་བྱང་།, is the translators' colophon. The Dzongsar text lacks it; on the vault owner's instruction (2026-10-03) it is added to the Tibetan from the same proofread page as row 33 (`supplement_rows` in the manifest, provenance in the block's sidecar entry). The Sanskrit and Chinese have no counterpart, so they show sections 1–2 only.
